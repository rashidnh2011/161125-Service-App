# 📚 **BizOps360 CRM User Training Manual**

## 🎯 **Welcome to BizOps360 CRM**

**BizOps360 CRM** is a comprehensive Customer Relationship Management system designed specifically for field service businesses. It helps sales teams manage leads, track opportunities, log activities, schedule visits, and analyze performance - all from a single, user-friendly platform.

---

## 🚀 **Getting Started**

### **System Access**
1. **Open your web browser** and navigate to your company URL
2. **Login** using your credentials provided by your administrator
3. **Select "CRM Dashboard"** from the main navigation menu

### **User Roles & Access Levels**

| Role | Access Level | Key Features |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Salesperson** | Full CRM Access | All CRM features, lead management, opportunity tracking |
| **Admin** | System + CRM | User management, analytics, system configuration |
| **Technician** | Limited CRM View | Customer info access, service coordination |

## 🎯 **Lead Management**

### **Creating a New Lead**
1. Navigate to **Lead Management**
2. Click **"Add New Lead"** button
3. Fill in the required information:
   - **First Name** & **Last Name** (required)
   - **Company** name
   - **Email** & **Phone** numbers
   - **Source** (where they heard about you)
   - **Priority** level
   - **Estimated Value**
   - **Expected Close Date**
4. Click **"Save Lead"**

### **Managing Existing Leads**
- **View Details**: Click on any lead to see full information
- **Edit Information**: Use the edit button to update details
- **Change Status**: Update lead status as it progresses
- **Assign Leads**: Assign leads to specific salespeople
- **Add Notes**: Keep detailed notes on customer interactions

### **Lead Status Workflow**
```
New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won/Lost
```

## 👥 **Contact Management**

### **Adding Contacts to Leads**
1. Open a lead's detail page
2. Go to the **"Contacts"** tab
3. Click **"Add Contact"**
4. Enter contact information:
   - Name and position
   - Contact details
   - Department
   - Mark as **"Primary Contact"** if applicable

### **Contact Features**
- **Multiple Contacts**: Add unlimited contacts per lead
- **Contact Roles**: Track different decision-makers
- **Communication History**: View all interactions with each contact

## 💼 **Opportunity Tracking**

### **Creating Opportunities**
1. From a **qualified lead**, click **"Convert to Opportunity"**
2. Or create manually in **Opportunity Tracking**
3. Set **opportunity details**:
   - Opportunity title
   - Expected value
   - Close date
   - Sales stage

### **Sales Pipeline Stages**
1. **Prospecting** - Initial research and identification
2. **Qualification** - Confirm needs and budget
3. **Proposal** - Present solution and pricing
4. **Negotiation** - Finalize terms and conditions
5. **Closed Won** - Successful sale completed
6. **Closed Lost** - Opportunity not pursued

### **Managing Opportunities**
- **Stage Updates**: Move opportunities through the pipeline
- **Value Tracking**: Monitor potential and actual revenue
- **Probability Assessment**: Update win probability
- **Notes & Attachments**: Keep detailed records

## 🚗 **Advanced Visit Tracking**

### **GPS-Enabled Field Visit Management**

The **Visit Tracking** system provides comprehensive field visit management with real-time GPS tracking and advanced workflow capabilities.

### **Visit Types**
- **Lead Visits**: Scheduled visits with existing leads
- **Non-Lead Visits**: Prospecting visits with potential customers
- **Support Visits**: Post-sale customer support

### **Starting a Visit**
1. Click **"Start Visit"** button
2. Select **Visit Type** (Lead or Non-Lead)
3. For **Non-Lead visits**, enter prospect details:
   - Prospect name (required)
   - Contact information (phone, email)
   - Company name
4. Enter **visit purpose** and **initial notes**
5. **GPS location** is automatically captured
6. Click **"Start Visit"** to begin tracking

### **Live Visit Monitoring**
- **Real-time Duration Tracking**: See live visit duration
- **Current Location**: GPS coordinates captured automatically
- **Visit Status**: Active indicator with pulsing animation
- **Update Notes**: Add notes during the visit
- **End Visit**: Complete visit with final notes

### **Advanced Features**

#### **GPS Location Tracking**
- **Start Location**: Captures coordinates when visit begins
- **End Location**: Records location when visit ends
- **Location Accuracy**: Shows coordinate precision
- **Address Resolution**: Attempts to resolve coordinates to addresses

#### **Visit Workflow Management**
```
Planned → In Progress → Completed/Cancelled
```

#### **Non-Lead to Lead Conversion**
1. Complete a **Non-Lead visit**
2. Click **"Convert to Lead"** button
3. Fill in complete lead information:
   - Personal details (name, contact info)
   - Company information
   - Lead source and notes
4. **Automatically creates** new lead from visit data

#### **Visit Documentation**
- **Purpose Tracking**: What was the visit objective?
- **Outcome Recording**: What was accomplished?
- **Follow-up Scheduling**: Next steps and timeline
- **Notes & Attachments**: Detailed visit records

### **Visit Status Management**
- **Planned**: Visit scheduled but not started
- **In Progress**: Currently active visit (live tracking)
- **Completed**: Successfully finished visit
- **Cancelled**: Visit cancelled before completion

### **Mobile Visit Features**
- **Offline Capability**: Start visits even without internet
- **Background Tracking**: Continues tracking when app minimized
- **Battery Optimization**: Efficient GPS usage
- **Emergency Stop**: Cancel visits if needed

### **Visit Analytics Integration**
- **Duration Analysis**: Track time spent per visit
- **Location Patterns**: Identify optimal visit areas
- **Success Rates**: Measure conversion from visits to leads
- **Performance Metrics**: Visit completion and follow-up rates

### **Best Practices for Visit Tracking**

#### **Pre-Visit Preparation**
1. **Plan Route**: Organize visits geographically
2. **Check Customer Details**: Review lead information
3. **Prepare Materials**: Have necessary documents ready
4. **Set Objectives**: Clear goals for each visit

#### **During Visit**
1. **Start Tracking**: Begin GPS tracking immediately
2. **Take Notes**: Record key discussion points
3. **Document Outcomes**: Note decisions and next steps
4. **Schedule Follow-ups**: Set dates for next interactions

#### **Post-Visit**
1. **Complete Documentation**: Final notes and outcomes
2. **Convert Prospects**: Turn promising visits into leads
3. **Update Opportunities**: Move sales process forward
4. **Schedule Activities**: Set follow-up tasks

### **Visit Tracking Tips**
- **Consistent Usage**: Start and end every visit properly
- **Location Permissions**: Ensure GPS access is enabled
- **Battery Management**: Close other apps during long visits
- **Data Sync**: Sync data when internet available
- **Emergency Protocols**: Know how to cancel visits if needed

### **Troubleshooting Visit Tracking**
- **Location Issues**: Check GPS permissions and internet
- **App Crashes**: Restart app and resync data
- **Missing Visits**: Check visit history and filters
- **Conversion Problems**: Verify lead creation permissions

## 📞 **Activity Logging**

### **Comprehensive Customer Interaction Tracking**

The Activity Logging system tracks all customer touchpoints and ensures no interaction is missed.

### **Activity Types**
- **📞 Calls**: Phone conversations and discussions
- **📧 Emails**: Email communications and correspondence
- **🤝 Meetings**: In-person or virtual meetings
- **🎯 Demos**: Product demonstrations and presentations
- **📋 Proposals**: Quote presentations and negotiations
- **🔄 Follow-ups**: Scheduled follow-up activities

### **Creating Activities**
1. Navigate to **Activity Logging**
2. Click **"Log Activity"** or **"Schedule Activity"**
3. Select **activity type** and **priority level**
4. Link to **relevant records** (leads, opportunities, contacts)
5. Set **scheduled date** and **description**
6. Add **location** if applicable

### **Activity Management**
- **Status Tracking**: Scheduled → Completed/Cancelled
- **Priority Levels**: Low, Medium, High importance
- **Due Date Reminders**: Automated notifications
- **Completion Tracking**: Mark activities as done

### **Integration with Other Modules**
- **Linked Records**: Connect activities to leads/opportunities
- **Visit Integration**: Log activities during visits
- **Calendar View**: See all scheduled activities
- **History Tracking**: Complete activity audit trail
